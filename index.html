    How to play Ayo

    1. The near row of six pits is yours. Each starts with 4 seeds.
    2. On your turn, pick one of your pits. All its seeds are sown one by one into the pits after it, going anticlockwise: along your row to the right, then back along Baba's row.
    3. If your last seed lands in one of Baba's pits and makes it 2 or 3, you capture them. Then check the pit before it: if that's also Baba's and holds 2 or 3, take those too, and so on.
    4. A pit with 12 or more seeds goes all the way round, and skips the pit it started from.
    5. You can't take every seed Baba has in one move. If you try, you take nothing.
    6. If Baba's row is empty, you must give him seeds if you can.
    7. The first to 25 seeds wins. If nobody can capture for a long time, each player keeps the seeds on their own side.

    On Easy and Normal, pointing at (or tapping) one of your pits shows where its last seed will land and what it would capture.
