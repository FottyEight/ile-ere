## Notes

- Freesound sources were taken from the public high-quality MP3 previews (128 kbps) of each sound, not the original WAV/FLAC uploads, then re-encoded. Kenney sources are the original OGG files from the packs.
- Encoding: MP3 (LAME via ffmpeg), 44.1 kHz. One-shots are mono 96 kbps with leading silence trimmed and peaks around -3 dBFS. Ambience loops are stereo 64 kbps, the generator loop is mono 64 kbps. Music is stereo 96 kbps.
- Loops (`amb_*_loop.mp3`, `generator_hum_loop.mp3`) were cut with an equal-power crossfade at the loop point. They were encoded with extra wrap-around audio before and after, and that extra audio is marked as encoder delay/padding in the LAME header, so decoders that honour the LAME gapless header (e.g. ffmpeg-based ones such as Chrome) return exactly the loop length without a click at the seam. Decoders that ignore the header play about 50 ms of matching wrap-around audio at each end, which is harmless.
- `music_1.mp3` and `music_2.mp3` are 2 min 20 s excerpts (from the start, faded out) of longer highlife songs by Ugo Mbaise, a highlife artist from Mbaise, Imo State, Nigeria. Lyrics were not reviewed.

Total size: 4917050 bytes (effects/foley/ambience 1555406 bytes, music 3361644 bytes).
